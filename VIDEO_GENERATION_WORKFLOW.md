## 📝 DETAILED WORKFLOW FOR DIY PRODUCTION

### **Phase 1: Pre-Production (1 Day)**

1. **Gather Your Team:**
   - You (director/producer)
   - Friend/family member (actor/talent)
   - Optional: Voiceover artist

2. **Scout Locations:**
   - Modern workspace (your home/office)
   - Rooftop with city view
   - Local shops for services scenes
   - Freelancer workspace

3. **Prepare Equipment:**
   - Charge camera/phone
   - Test lighting setup
   - Check audio recording
   - Create shot list

4. **Collect Assets:**
   - Download royalty-free music
   - Download sound effects
   - Find stock footage for placeholder scenes

---

### **Phase 2: Production (2-3 Days)**

#### **Shoot Scene 1 (Opening Hook) - 30 minutes**
- Film creator at desk with phone/laptop
- Do 5+ takes from different angles
- Close-ups of hands, face, and objects
- Use your smartphone or camera at 1080p minimum

#### **Shoot Scene 2 (Freelancing) - 45 minutes**
- Film typing on laptop
- Screen record graphic design/editing software
- Show notification on phone
- Get close-ups of hands, screen content

#### **Shoot Scene 3 (Digital Products) - 30 minutes**
- Screen record Canva/design tool
- Show e-books, templates being created
- Record phone screen showing digital products
- Do transitions between screens

#### **Shoot Scene 4 (Social Media) - 45 minutes**
- Record Instagram/social media content creation
- Show phone notifications
- Film person managing content
- Get screen recordings of social apps

#### **Shoot Scene 5 (Short-Form Content) - 1 hour**
- Set up phone on tripod
- Record yourself creating content
- Record editing in CapCut/Adobe
- Screen record upload process

#### **Shoot Scene 6 (Local Services) - 1-2 hours**
- Film tutoring scenario
- Cleaning/gardening (if available)
- Tech help (older person with younger person)
- Food delivery/service scenario
- May need multiple locations/days

#### **Shoot Scene 7 (Rooftop) - 45 minutes**
- Find rooftop with city view
- Shoot during golden hour (sunset/sunrise)
- Get smooth camera movement shots
- Multiple angles and takes

#### **Voiceover Recording - 2 hours**
- Record in quiet room
- Speak clearly and naturally
- Record full script as one take
- Re-record sections that need work
- Aim for 55-60 seconds total

---

### **Phase 3: Post-Production (3-4 Days)**

#### **Step 1: Import & Organize** (1 hour)
1. Create new project in DaVinci Resolve/Premiere
   - Settings: 1080×1920, 30fps, 9:16 aspect ratio
2. Import all video clips into media bin
3. Create folders: Footage, Audio, Graphics, Music
4. Organize by scene (Scene1, Scene2, etc.)

#### **Step 2: Rough Cut** (2 hours)
1. Drag clips to timeline in scene order
2. Trim each clip to remove mistakes
3. Arrange for ~10 seconds per business idea
4. Arrange opening and closing scenes

#### **Step 3: Add Voiceover** (30 minutes)
1. Import voiceover audio file
2. Place on audio track
3. Adjust levels to -3dB
4. Sync with video scenes (match cuts to pauses)

#### **Step 4: Add Sound Effects** (1 hour)
Reference table from Production Guide:
1. Import all SFX files
2. Place at correct timestamps
3. Set volumes to -8 to -10dB (below voiceover)
4. Use fade-in/fade-out for smooth transitions

#### **Step 5: Add Background Music** (1 hour)
1. Import royalty-free music track
2. Place on music track
3. Set volume to -12dB (below voiceover)
4. Adjust music dynamics for each scene:
   - Strong at 0-3s (opening hook)
   - Peak energy at 23-43s (content creation scenes)
   - Inspirational at 51-56s (closing)
   - Fade out at 56-60s

#### **Step 6: Add Text/Captions** (2 hours)
Using DaVinci Resolve's Fusion or Premiere Pro's Essential Graphics:

**Scene 1 (0-3s):**
- Text: "5 BUSINESSES" (scale + bounce in)
- Text: "ALMOST ₹0" (scale + bounce, highlight ₹0)

**Scene 2 (3-13s):**
- Text: "1. FREELANCING" (slide in from left)
- Text: "Writing • Design • Editing • Coding" (staggered)

**Scene 3 (13-23s):**
- Text: "2. DIGITAL PRODUCTS" (slide in)
- Text: "E-books • Templates • Notes" (3D flip effect)

**Scene 4 (23-33s):**
- Text: "3. SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGEMENT" (bounce in)
- Text: "Create • Post • Manage" (stagger)

**Scene 5 (33-43s):**
- Text: "4. SHORT-FORM CONTENT" (camera shutter effect)
- Text: "YouTube • Shorts • Reels" (play button animation)

**Scene 6 (43-51s):**
- Text: "5. LOCAL SERVICES" (gradient animation)
- Text: "Skill + Local Demand"

**Scene 7 (51-56s):**
- Text: "START SMALL." (emphasis)
- Text: "THINK BIG." (aspirational)

**Scene 8 (56-60s):**
- Text: "Which one would YOU start?" (question animation)
- Text: "Comment below"
- Text: "SUBSCRIBE — HARSHVERSE" (with subscribe button animation)

**Text Formatting Guidelines:**
- Font: Poppins or Montserrat (modern sans-serif)
- Size: 60-80px (readable on phone)
- Color: White with -20 black shadow, OR neon green/yellow for key words
- Position: Safe area (1/6 from edges, avoid UI)
- Duration: 2-4 seconds minimum per text
- Animation: Slide, bounce, fade, scale (smooth, not gimmicky)

#### **Step 7: Color Grading** (1-2 hours)
1. Apply LUT (Look-Up Table) for cinematic feel:
   - Try "Creamy" or "Warm Cinema" LUT
   - Adjust: Shadows (+10), Highlights (-10), Saturation (+5%)
2. Individual scene adjustments:
   - Boost indoor scenes with warm tint
   - Keep outdoor golden hour natural
   - Ensure skin tones are warm but natural
3. Add subtle vignette (10-15% opacity)
4. Increase contrast by 5-10%

#### **Step 8: Transitions** (1 hour)
Between scenes, add smooth transitions:
- **0-3s to 3-13s:** Whip transition + whoosh sound
- **Between business ideas:** Quick cut or subtle dissolve (200ms)
- **43-51s to 51-56s:** Fade transition
- **56-60s:** Smooth fade to black

Avoid transitions that are too flashy.

#### **Step 9: Final Audio Mix** (1 hour)
1. Set voiceover to -3dB
2. Set music to -12dB
3. Set SFX to -8dB
4. Use compressor on voiceover (ratio 4:1, threshold -20dB)
5. Add subtle EQ: boost 3kHz (presence), cut 200Hz (mud)
6. Normalize final mix to -1dB peak

#### **Step 10: Final Review** (30 minutes)
- Watch full video top to bottom
- Check timing accuracy
- Verify text readability
- Check audio sync
- Ensure no copyright issues with music
- Make final adjustments

---

### **Phase 4: Export (30 minutes)**

#### **Export Settings (For YouTube Shorts):**

**DaVinci Resolve:**
1. File → Export → Add to Render Queue
2. Format: MP4
3. Codec: H.264
4. Resolution: 1080 × 1920
5. Frame rate: 30fps
6. Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps (high quality)
7. Audio: 48kHz, 192kbps stereo
8. Save as: `HarshVerse_5Businesses_Short.mp4`

**Adobe Premiere Pro:**
1. File → Export → Media
2. Format: H.264
3. Preset: YouTube 1080p (then modify)
4. Resolution: 1080 × 1920
5. Frame rate: 30fps
6. Bitrate: 12 Mbps (VBR, 2-pass)
7. Audio: 48kHz, stereo, 192kbps
8. Export

**Rendering Time:** 5-15 minutes (depends on computer)
