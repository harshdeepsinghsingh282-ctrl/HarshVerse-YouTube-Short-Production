## 🎬 VISUAL STYLE GUIDELINES

### Color Grading:
- **Overall:** Slightly warm, cinematic look (not oversaturated)
- **Skin Tone:** Natural, warm but not orange
- **Contrast:** High but not harsh
- **Saturation:** 105–110% (slightly boosted for energy)
- **LUT:** Modern, Indian cinema-inspired color grade

### Camera Work:
- **Aperture:** F/2–F/4 (shallow depth of field for cinematic look)
- **Shutter Speed:** 1/60 or 1/50 (motion blur for smoothness)
- **Movements:** Smooth dollies, slow pushes, minimal shakes
- **Transitions:** Whip pans, J-cuts, match cuts

### Text/Captions:
- **Font:** Modern sans-serif (Montserrat, Poppins, or similar)
- **Size:** Large enough to read on phone (minimum 40px)
- **Color:** White with subtle shadow OR bright accent color (neon green/yellow for emphasis)
- **Animation:** Slide-in, bounce, scale, or fade (nothing too gimmicky)
- **Placement:** Safe area (1/6 from edges) to avoid YouTube UI overlap
- **Duration:** Each text stays 2–4 seconds minimum

### Effects:
- **Transitions:** Quick cuts, whip pans, subtle dissolves
- **Minimal VFX:** Only use authentic-looking effects (no cheesy animations)
- **Number Counters:** Smooth digital font, realistic timing (not too fast)
- **Glows/Highlights:** Subtle, cinematic (avoid neon excess)
